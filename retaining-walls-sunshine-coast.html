Cost bands by material

Prices are calculated by square metre of wall face, not linear metres. Indicative bands run from around $150 to $300 per square metre for rock and boulder walls, $300 to $450 for timber sleepers, $250 to $430 for gabion, $550 to $700 for concrete sleeper, and $550 to $750 for core filled block. These bands exclude GST, excavation, drainage, imported backfill, engineering and certification. When comparing quotes, ask for the wall face cost to be separated from these exclusions to ensure an accurate comparison. A wall 1.2 metres high and 10 metres long equals 12 square metres of face, not 10 metres of wall. This method makes quote comparisons far more accurate than asking for a rate per metre without the height.

Drainage and engineering

Drainage is treated as a structural requirement, not a finishing touch. Poor drainage is the leading cause of wall failure. Every wall should include ag drain, aggregate and geofabric behind it. For wetter hinterland slopes and coastal sand, this detail belongs in the main scope, not as an afterthought. Engineered walls over 1 metre or carrying a surcharge are designed to AS 4678. The design is typically certified by an RPEQ via Form 15 for the design and Form 16 on inspection. This ensures the wall can safely hold the ground and any surcharge load. A repair quote should test what sits behind the wall now, whether the drainage path still works and whether the existing structure matches the load it holds. retaining walls sunshine experts treat drainage as a core structural element.

Choosing the right wall type

Concrete sleeper walls between galvanised posts are the workhorse option for replacement work on cut and fill blocks. Timber sleeper terraces suit a low garden fall. Core filled besser block handles taller engineered walls and rendered finishes. Rock and boulder walls suit acreage sites with room behind the face, where a battered profile can sit back into the slope. Gabion baskets are identified for scour and surface runoff on erodible ground. Match the wall type to the room, runoff and slope behaviour of your site. If two materials still look plausible, compare how much space exists behind the face, whether a vertical finish is necessary and whether the wall is replacing a failed structure.

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a retaining wall? Sunshine Coast Council requires a building permit before construction, modification or repair of most retaining walls, with concurrence for walls over 2 metres.

How are retaining walls priced? They are priced by square metre of wall face, which is height multiplied by length. Bands range from $150 to $750 depending on material.

What causes retaining walls to fail? Poor drainage is the leading cause of wall failure. Ag drain, aggregate and geofabric must be installed behind every wall to allow water to escape.
